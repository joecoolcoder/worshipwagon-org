Monthly Themes for 2026

Fruits of the Spirit

January - Love

We can love because He first loved us. The Bible reveals the source of love and the true nature of love.

Related Passages: Exodus 34:6; Mathew 19:19; Mathew 22:37; John 15:12

February - Joy

As followers of Jesus, we have an opportunity to experience joy, even though we have trials and hardships. This joy has been made available for all who follow Jesus.

Related Passages: Psalm 16:11; Psalm 30:5; Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 61:7; Luke 2:10

March - Peace

One of the greatest things to have in life is peace. God grants peace to those who seek Him and live according to His ways.

Related Passages: Ps. 4:8; Ps. 34:14; Luke 1:79; John 14:27

April - Patience

With online orders being delivered within 24 hours, instant prints at the photo store, and fast food everywhere, it’s no surprise that our society struggles with being patient. Learning to be patient can help improve the quality of our life and relationships.

Related Passages: Ps. 37:7-9; Ro. 12:12; Gal. 6:9; Eph. 4:2

May - Kindness

Little acts of kindness can make our day. As we recognize God’s incredible kindness in our lives, we can be freer in exercising kindness to others.

Related Passages: Ps. 17:7; Ps. 143:8; Isa. 63:7; Col. 3:12

June - Goodness

The news tends to be full of “bad” things that are happening around the world. When we yield our lives to God, we become agents of “goodness” which will triumph over the bad.

Related Passages: Ps. 27:13; Ps. 34:8; Ro. 12:9; Gal. 6:10

July - Faithfulness

The fruit of the Spirit is what takes place in the life of a person who becomes a follower of Christ. The first fruit mentioned in the book of Galatians is “faithfulness.” We must remember God is always faithful and examine ways we can become more faithful.

Related Passages: Gal. 5:22-23; Deut. 28; Prov. 28:20; Luke 16:10-12; 2 Cor. 5:7

August - Gentleness

Gentleness may sometimes be overlooked as one of the fruit of the Spirit; however, this spiritual attribute can have an incredible affect on the believer’s life.

Related Passages: Psalms 1:35; Prov. 15:1; Mt. 11:29; Titus 3:2

September - Self Control

In a society that tends to spin out of control, this fruit of the Spirit can bring stability to the follower of Christ and those around him/her.

Related Passages: Prov. 16:32; Prov. 25:28; I Cor. 10:13; 2 Peter 1:5-7

October - Humility

This seems to be a rare trait these days. Humility is not thinking less about yourself but thinking about yourself less. It involves a recognition of God’s grace and mercy in your life. Those who are humble can often be seen taking an active interest in other people’s well-being. Some have described it as being respectful of others, aware of your own shortcomings, and dependent on God’s grace for your life.

Related Passages: Psalm 25:9; Psalm 147: 6; James 4:6

November - Compassion

Another word for compassion is love. God demonstrates this in the most famous verse of all, John 3:16. We can find great peace in knowing that God’s compassion never ends and that He is always the same. He never changes. This characteristic of God has been fundamental to the Jewish and Christian Faith.

Related Passages: Psalm 103:13; Isaiah 54:10; John 3:16

December - Love Revisited

There are three types of love mentioned in the Bible. They are Eros (sensual love), Phileo (brotherly love), and Agape (Divine/Perfect) Love. Although the first two forms of love are wonderful when expressed in the right way, the highest form of Love is Agape. This is the love that is unconditional and reflects God’s love for us. This form of love is sacrificial. It gives and expects nothing in return.

Related Passages: 1 Corinthians 13
