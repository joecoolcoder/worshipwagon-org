Monthly Themes for 2026

Fruits of the Spirit

January - Love

We can love because He first loved us. The Bible reveals the source of love and the true nature of love.

Related passages: Exodus 34:6; Matthew 19:19; Matthew 22:37; John 15:12.

February - Joy

As followers of Jesus, we have an opportunity to experience joy, even though we have trials and hardships. This joy has been made available for all who follow Jesus.

Related passages: Psalm 16:11; Psalm 30:5; Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 61:7; Luke 2:10.

March - Peace

One of the greatest things to have in life is peace. God grants peace to those who seek Him and live according to His ways.

Related passages: Psalm 4:8; Psalm 34:14; Luke 1:79; John 14:27.

April - Patience

With online orders, instant prints, and fast food everywhere, it is no surprise that our society struggles with being patient. Learning to be patient can help improve the quality of our life and relationships.

Related passages: Psalm 37:7-9; Romans 12:12; Galatians 6:9; Ephesians 4:2.

May - Kindness

Little acts of kindness can make our day. As we recognize God's incredible kindness in our lives, we can be freer in exercising kindness to others.

Related passages: Psalm 17:7; Psalm 143:8; Isaiah 63:7; Colossians 3:12.

June - Goodness

When we yield our lives to God, we become agents of goodness which will triumph over the bad.

Related passages: Psalm 27:13; Psalm 34:8; Romans 12:9; Galatians 6:10.

July - Faithfulness

The fruit of the Spirit is what takes place in the life of a person who becomes a follower of Christ. We must remember God is always faithful and examine ways we can become more faithful.

Related passages: Galatians 5:22-23; Deuteronomy 28; Proverbs 28:20; Luke 16:10-12; 2 Corinthians 5:7.

August - Gentleness

Gentleness may sometimes be overlooked as one of the fruit of the Spirit; however, this spiritual attribute can have an incredible effect on the believer's life.

Related passages: Psalm 1:35; Proverbs 15:1; Matthew 11:29; Titus 3:2.

September - Self Control

In a society that tends to spin out of control, this fruit of the Spirit can bring stability to the follower of Christ and those around him or her.

Related passages: Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 25:28; 1 Corinthians 10:13; 2 Peter 1:5-7.

October - Humility

Humility is not thinking less about yourself but thinking about yourself less. It involves a recognition of God's grace and mercy in your life.

Related passages: Psalm 25:9; Psalm 147:6; James 4:6.

November - Compassion

Another word for compassion is love. We can find great peace in knowing that God's compassion never ends and that He is always the same.

Related passages: Psalm 103:13; Isaiah 54:10; John 3:16.

December - Love Revisited

Although many forms of love are wonderful when expressed in the right way, the highest form of love is agape. This is unconditional love that reflects God's love for us.

Related passage: 1 Corinthians 13.
