Inclement Weather Guidelines

We strive to be consistent and reliable, generally always trying to find a way to hold services each and every scheduled night. We have endured blistering heat, thunderstorms, snow, gusty winds, and bitter cold. We have found that the number of times we need to cancel services has been one or two days each year at most.

That being said, certain weather conditions may create unnecessary risk for our volunteers, our homeless friends, or both. We use the following rules of thumb when the weather is not looking great:

  • If the temperature is forecast to be less than 30 degrees, we hold a shortened service and do not include a live music portion.
  • If the temperature is forecast to be less than 10 degrees, we cancel all services.
  • If there is a winter storm warning in effect for the time of service, we cancel all services.
  • If there is a severe thunderstorm warning in effect for the time of service, we cancel all services.
  • If there is a tornado warning in effect for the time of service, we cancel all services.
  • For locations that are not protected from rain and snow, services may be canceled for moderate or heavy rain, snow or ice covering the ground, or single digit wind chill temperatures.
