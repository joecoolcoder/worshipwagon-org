Monthly Themes

Core Christian Topics

January - Defining the Gospel

The Good News (Gospel) of Jesus Christ is the main theme throughout Scripture. It’s vital that we all understand the basics of the Gospel.

Related Passages: Isaiah 61:1-4; Romans Road (Romans 3:23, 6:23; 5:8; 10:9-12); John 3:16&17

February - Grace

Grace is God’s unmerited favor. It is kindness from God we don’t deserve. There is nothing we have done, nor can ever do, to earn this favor. The Bible teaches us that God’s love and forgiveness is a free gift, and we are wise to receive it as such.

Related Passages: Proverbs 3:34; John 1:17Acts 15:11; Romans 3:24; Romans 5:172; Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 2:5

March - Forgiveness

The importance of receiving God’s forgiveness cannot be understated. Likewise, the benefits of forgiving others will bring health and freedom to those who give it freely.

Related Passages: Mathew 6:14-15; 1 John 1:9; Isaiah 43:25-26; Micah 7:18-19; Mark 11:25

April - Character of God

It’s important to know God’s character. Without knowing God’s character, it’s hard to understand His ways in our life. Knowing God’s Character also helps us trust in God.

Related Passages: Justice: Psalm 33:5; Goodness: Psalm 34:8; Patient: Psalm 103:8, 1 Timothy 1:16; Merciful: Exodus 34:6, 2 Corinthians 1:3

May - Salvation

This theme has eternal consequences. Understanding how we are saved is at the core of the faith. It takes more than head knowledge. Receiving Christ as Lord and Savior becomes central to salvation.

Related Passages: Mathew 8:18-22; Mathew 19:25&26; Romans 8:38&39; Revelations 3:20

June - Perseverance

One of the common trademarks of all the Saints mentioned in Hebrews 11 was perseverance. By the grace of God, followers of God can persevere through the various trials and seasons of life.

Related Passages: Psalm 27:15; Romans 8:37-39; Romans 12:12; Hebrew 6:11-15

July - Prayer

Prayer has been a trademark of all the heroes of the faith. They have recognized it as a lifeline to God. It offers us the opportunity to have a relationship with the living God. Prayer does change things – especially for those who pray.

Related Passages: Mathew 7:7; John 14:13-14; James 5:14-16; Philippians 4:6-7

August - Family of God

It’s easy for all people to feel isolated and disconnected; however, God has made a way for us to be brought into his family. This is good news for all people.

Related Passages: John 1:22; Ephesians 2:19-22; 2 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:1-21

September - Hope

We have an inheritance reserved in heaven, a living hope through the resurrection of Christ from the dead.

Related Passages: 1 Peter 1:3-5; Psalm 42:11; Psalm 62:5; Mathew 12:21

October - Mercy

Mercy is the kissing cousin of Grace. Grace has been defined as receiving the good things of God that we don’t deserve. Likewise, Mercy has been defined as not receiving the things we do deserve (like God’s judgement and wrath). Mercy has also been described as God’s kindness, compassion, and forgiveness. The Scriptures are clear that we have a merciful God, and we are to emulate Him.

Related Passages: Ephesians 2:3; Luke 1:78; Exodus 33:19; Exodus 34:6-7; Romans 9:16, 22-23

November - Ordinary People Doing the Extraordinary

Throughout history, including Biblical history, we see that God uses ordinary people to accomplish His will. We should never underestimate what God can do through someone that yields their life to God.

Related Passages: Prov. 16:3; Luke 1:37; Eph. 3:20; Phil. 4:13

December - Heaven

It’s all too easy to become so earthly minded that we forget that Heaven is a literal place that is so much better than anything we have experienced or can even imagine. Indeed, earth is the changing room for glory (Heaven).

Related Passages: John 14:2; I Cor. 2:7-9; Phil. 3:20-21; Rev. 21:4; Rev. 22:1-5
